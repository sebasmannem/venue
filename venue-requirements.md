# PGConf.EU Venue Requirements (summary)

Condensed from the three `PGConf.EU Venue Requirements 2027*.pdf` files, to check candidate
venues against. The **Complete** document is the authoritative one; the requirement IDs
(C1, A1, R1, …) below refer to it. Points that only appear in, or differ in, the older
short spec (`PGConf.EU Venue Requirements 2027.pdf`) are marked as such.

## At a glance

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Attendees | 700–800 expected; rooms sized for up to 900 |
| When | Late October (preferably 2nd–4th week), avoiding local and common European holidays |
| Days | 4 event days, preferably Tue–Fri (alternative Mon–Thu), plus partial Day 0 (set-up) and Day 5 (tear-down) |
| Plenary | Up to 900, theatre style |
| Breakouts (Days 1–3) | 3 × 300+ and 1 × 200+, theatre or classroom |
| Community day (Day 4) | 5–6 rooms of 50–100 (e.g. 3 × 50 + 3 × 100), classroom, up to 300 people + ~12 staff |
| Other rooms | Hacker lounge (~25), quiet room (6–8), lockable office ≥ 25 m² (Day 0–5) |
| Sponsors | 12–15 stands of ~3 × 3–3.5 m near catering |
| Registration | ~6 m desk near main entrance, 1.5 m storage behind, power |
| Wi-Fi | Up to 1,800 devices, custom SSID, no captive portal; own routers allowed (**hard requirement**) |
| Power | ~120 multi-socket strips installed by venue |
| Catering | Drinks all day, 2 coffee breaks, buffet lunch Days 1–4 (700–900 / up to 300), all at once in ~1 h |
| Accessibility | Step-free everywhere, accessible toilets on every floor, accessible seating |
| Accommodation | Conference hotel rate Sun–Sun (hotel venues) or nearby hotel suggestions (congress centres) |

Past editions for reference: Valencia 2026 (~750, Palacio de Congresos), Riga 2025 (675),
Athens 2024 (782), Prague 2023 (722), Berlin 2022 (604). The last Amsterdam edition was
2011 (Casa 400, 188 attendees).

## Schedule and access

| Day | Access needed | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 (partial) | Office from 08:00; common areas (foyers, registration, exhibition, lounges) from 12:00 until at least 19:00 | Office/registration set-up, deliveries, stands built, sponsors set up from 12:00. Venue sets up and tests AV/staging/seating in talk rooms before Day 1. No catering. |
| Days 1–3 | 07:00–19:00 (later on reception evening) | Main conference: plenary, 4 parallel tracks, sponsor exhibition. Sponsors on stands from 07:00. |
| Day 4 | 07:30–19:00 | Community day: smaller workshops/sessions |
| Day 5 (partial) | Office 08:00–13:00 | Collection of sponsor and organiser shipments. No catering. |

- **A1** Day 0 access included; talk rooms fully set and tested before doors open on Day 1.
- **A2** Any staff needed for early/late access, Day 0 or Day 5 must be quoted.
- **A3** No other event in our rooms on Day 0 afternoon and Days 1–4; disclose other events in the building, which shared areas they use, and how catering is kept separate.
- **A4** Room changeovers (e.g. theatre → classroom overnight) quoted, incl. porter hours.

## Spaces

### Conference days (Days 1–3)

| Space | Capacity / layout | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plenary | 700–900, theatre | May be the same space as breakouts if partitioning fits in a 30-min break (incl. people leaving and re-entering). State who partitions and whether it costs. |
| Breakout rooms | 3 × 300+ and 1 × 200+, theatre or classroom | Some flexibility on sizes |
| Hacker lounge | ~25, mix of tables and sofas | Power at every table |
| Quiet room | 6–8, comfortable seating | Away from the buzz; for rest/recovery, not meetings |
| Speaker ready area *(optional)* | 6–10 | Table, power, HDMI monitor; may share the organiser office |
| Breakfast / side-event room *(optional)* | 40–60, round tables | Early morning on one day; quote separately |
| Sponsor meeting room *(optional)* | ~10, boardroom | Bookable by sponsors, if space allows |
| Childcare room *(optional, short spec only)* | Up to 12 children + carers | Minimal tables for activities/eating |

- **R1** Theatre counts must be real chairs after stage, AV desk, cameras and fire aisles — not brochure maximums.
- **R2** State which rooms are shared with or visible to other events.
- **R3** Room names, capacities and floor plan in the response.

### Community day (Day 4)

- 5–6 rooms, minimum 50 each in classroom, flexible (e.g. 3 × 50 + 3 × 100); up to 300 attendees + ~12 staff.
- Power: one 3-socket strip per 3 seats.
- Catering: coffee, water, lunch for up to 300.
- **D1** Layouts final ~4 weeks before; state change deadline and cost after it.
- **D2** Overnight changeover of reused Day 1–3 rooms must be quoted.

### All days (Day 0–5)

- **L1** Lockable organiser office, ≥ 25 m², with tables, chairs and power, Day 0 morning to Day 5 midday.

## Audio visual, staging and recording

| Item | Plenary | Breakouts (Days 1–3) | Community day rooms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Projection / screens | Readable for 900 | Readable from any seat | Readable from any seat |
| Laptop input | HDMI + USB-C adapter | HDMI + USB-C adapter | HDMI |
| Laptop audio | 3.5 mm jack at lectern | 3.5 mm jack at lectern | Not required |
| Microphones | 2 handheld + 2 lavalier | 1 handheld + 1 lavalier | 1 handheld + 1 lavalier where amplification needed (short spec: the two larger rooms) |
| Mixer, amp, speakers | Yes | Yes | Where mics are used |
| Confidence monitor | Preferred | Preferred | Not required |
| Stage / lectern | Stage, lectern, stage lighting | Lectern; stage if needed for sightlines | Lectern or table |

- Speakers bring their own laptops.
- **AV1** Per room: what's installed vs. added for us, with cost of each addition.
- **AV2** Stage, head table, stools, lighting: included or cost, per room.
- **AV3** Enough technicians to respond in any talk room within minutes (1 per 2 nearby rooms is fine); state numbers and hours.
- **AV4** Clean line-level audio output from each room's mixer (for live captioning); state connector type.
- **AV5** Laptop changeovers ≤ 5 minutes.

### Video recording (optional, desirable for Days 1–3)

- **V1** One fixed speaker camera per room is enough.
- **V2** Audio from the sound desk, not the camera.
- **V3** One composed video per talk: our background frame, slides + speaker windows, our opening/closing slides (title, speaker, etc.).
- **V4** YouTube-ready 1920×1080 files, delivered electronically or on drives we keep; state delivery date.
- **V5** Examples: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PNohLdtVNYs>, <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7a1otYLZxy4>

### Other optional services

- **Live captioning** (we may run our own): per room, mixer audio output, power and a secure spot for a small computer, outbound internet (via our router, W6), and a 65" on-stage monitor with HDMI plus cabling. Quote monitor/cabling per room per day.
- **Simultaneous translation**: not expected, quote if offered.

## Power and furniture

~120 multi-socket strips, installed and removed by the venue, all labour included.

| Location | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Classroom rooms (mainly Day 4) | One 3-socket strip per 3 seats, cables taped/covered |
| Hacker lounge / lounge seating | One strip per table plus sofa areas |
| Registration desk | ≥ 4 sockets |
| Each sponsor stand | ≥ 3 sockets |
| Organiser office | ≥ 8 sockets |
| Speaker ready area (if provided) | ≥ 4 sockets |
| Plenary and breakout rooms | Sockets at lectern and front row |

- **P1** Quote power as one line per location (strips, distribution, cable covers, labour) — not just a unit rental price.
- **F1** List included furniture per room/area and prices for extras.
- **F2** Quote furniture carrying/set-up labour if charged.
- **F3** Lounge and quiet room furniture in the quote, with photos or model names.
- **F4** State stock limits (e.g. chair counts) that would force hire.

## Registration, signage and wayfinding

- **G1** Registration: 3 tables of 1 × 2 m (L shape OK) or equivalent counter, ≥ 1.5 m clear behind for boxes, power. Merchandise (T-shirts) is sold here; state any sales fee.
- **G2** Fixed counters: length, and whether a second area is available for merchandise.
- **G3** Included printed/digital signage, extra costs, per-day or per-event pricing, artwork deadlines.
- **G4** Digital signage updatable during the event; who does it and at what cost.
- **G5** May we put up our own banners/pull-ups; fixing rules.
- **G6** Flag poles: availability, number, price per flag per day.

## Sponsor exhibition

12–15 stands, all the same size, in high-traffic areas near catering; used Days 1–3 with set-up Day 0 afternoon.

| | Platinum | Gold |
|---|---|---|
| Number | 4–5 | 8–10 |
| Position | Highest-traffic spots | Other good-traffic spots |
| Furniture | ~2 × 1 m table + 2 chairs | ~2 × 1 m table + 2 chairs |
| Power | ≥ 3 sockets | ≥ 3 sockets |

> The older short spec used different sizes (Platinum ~5 × 2.5 m, Gold ~2.5 × 1.5 m); the Complete spec makes all stands ~3 × 3–3.5 m.

- **X1** Venue confirms the layout fits (fire exits, ramps, escape routes); levelling/adaptations costed.
- **X2** Footprint ~3 m wide × 3–3.5 m deep: table, staff/banner space behind, visitor space in front without blocking walkways.
- **X3** Mandatory stand items (e.g. carpet) and cost; carpet colour preferably PostgreSQL blue `#336791`.
- **X4** Sponsors may swap furniture (with our approval); prices and deadline.
- **X5** Sponsors may bring own LED/OLED screens (no projectors) and roll-ups, or hire screens; price list; disclose any restrictions/fees on sponsor equipment.
- **X6** Exhibitor handbook with all prices provided with the response.
- **X7** Sponsor access from 12:00 Day 0 and 07:00 Days 1–3.
- **X8** Sponsor extras ordered via us, but **billed directly to the sponsor**, never to PostgreSQL Europe.

## Deliveries, storage and handling

- **L2** Accept shipments for us and sponsors from ≥ 7 days before Day 0; store securely; state address, labelling, size/count limits (no truck-sized shipments).
- **L3** Either deliver to office/registration/stands before Day 0, or make available for collection with trolleys. Organiser handling in quote; sponsor handling billed to sponsors.
- **L4** Name any required/recommended freight forwarder with prices (sponsors deal with them directly).
- **L5** Customs requirements for non-EU shipments (EORI/tax ID, consignee).
- **L6** Return shipments collected from office/stands and held for courier; state duration and cost.
- **L7** Waste/cardboard removal charges and rules.

## Catering and evening reception

| Day | Covers | Service |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–3 | 700–900 | Hot/cold drinks all day from registration opening, water always, mid-morning and mid-afternoon breaks with snacks, buffet (or sit-down) lunch |
| Day 4 | Up to 300 | As above |
| Reception, Day 1 or 2 evening *(preferred, not required)* | All attendees | Bar + buffet snacks (not a full meal); drink tickets preferred over fully open bar |

- Lunch for all attendees at the same time, within a ~1 hour break.
- **K1** Exclusive/in-house caterer quoted with the proposal; state corkage/outside catering fees.
- **K2** Water provision (fountains, dispensers, etc.), number of points, cost per day; available all day.
- **K3** Vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, lactose-free and common allergens catered and labelled at no extra cost.
- **K4** Minimum numbers, final-number deadline, reduction charges (adjustable up to 7 days before, C6).
- **K5** Reception quoted separately: per head, drink tickets, bar staff, extended hours.

## Wi-Fi and network

Support 2 devices per attendee (up to 1,800), full cost in the quote.

- **W1** Custom SSID and password, no captive portal (strongly preferred).
- **W2** Total uplink bandwidth and per-device limits, stated realistically.
- **W3** Coverage in all rooms, foyers, exhibition, catering, lounges; 900 in the plenary at once. Payment terminals and ticket scanners use Wi-Fi.
- **W4** Cost of custom SSID / extra capacity, plus what's included as standard.
- **W5** Named network contact reachable during the event.
- **W6** **Required**: we may connect our own routers (for organiser equipment only, e.g. payment terminals, captioning), bypassing any captive portal. A venue that forbids this is **not acceptable**.

## Venue staff, safety and security

List every staff role on site, with numbers, hours and duties — including anything required by law, insurers or authorities.

| Role | What we need to know |
|---|---|
| Event coordinator | Named, on site all event days (C8) |
| AV technicians | Number, rooms covered, hours, whether they cover recording/signage (AV3) |
| Porters / set-up crew | Number, hours, included changeovers (A4) |
| Maintenance / electrician | Number, hours, whether power install is included (P1) |
| IT / network support | Whether they support our network during the event (W5) |
| Hostesses / floor staff | Number, hours, duties, whether we can direct them |
| Security | Whether required, hours (incl. Day 0, early/late), cost |
| Fire marshals / fire watch | Whether required, cost |
| First aid / medical | Whether required, cost |
| Cleaning | Included cleaning during/after each day; extras |

- **S1** Every known-mandatory role in the quote; unquoted mandatory roles won't be paid.
- **S2** Maximum occupancy per room and building; any safety briefing we must give.
- **S3** Insurance required from us (e.g. public liability, minimum cover).

## Accessibility, wellbeing and inclusion

- **Y1** Step-free access (no steps or escalators) to all rooms, foyers, exhibition, catering, lounges, toilets and reception venue; wheelchair-sized lifts in service throughout.
- **Y2** Accessible toilets on every floor used.
- **Y3** Reserved accessible seating in every talk room with clear sightline.
- **Y4** Quiet room (see Spaces).
- **Y5** Live captioning support if taken up (audio out, on-stage monitor, cabling, power, internet).
- **Y6** Assistance dogs welcome? Relief area nearby?

## Accommodation

Attendees book and pay for their own rooms; our liability for unsold rooms must be small and released late.

- **H1** Hotel venues: conference rate bookable via link/code on our website, at least Sunday before to Sunday after (smaller allocation outside main nights).
- **H2** Congress centres: suggest hotels within walking distance or short public transport, ideally offering a conference rate.
- **H3** Room-block guarantee, minimum nights, release/cut-off date (2–3 weeks before preferred), attrition terms.
- **H4** Per-night taxes and fees on top of the rate (e.g. city tax — relevant for Amsterdam).
- **H5** How rooms we book ourselves (speakers, diversity scholarships) are invoiced.

## Contractual requirements

- **C1** This document and the venue's itemised response are part of the signed agreement and prevail over conflicting terms unless agreed in writing.
- **C2** All foreseeable charges quoted: mandatory staff (fire, security, first aid, technicians, porters, cleaners…), labour for install/removal, out-of-hours access, mandatory items (stand carpet, cable covers…), exclusive suppliers, service/admin/card fees, taxes and VAT, waste removal, merchandise sales fees.
- **C3** Unquoted foreseeable charges are not payable; new charges only for written change requests with an accepted quote.
- **C4** Included staff itemised: role, number, hours per day, what they do and don't do.
- **C5** Single currency, VAT/taxes stated, quote valid ≥ 60 days, unit prices for common extras.
- **C6** Minimum spend, catering minimums, room-block guarantees, reduction dates; catering adjustable up to 7 days before without penalty; cancellation schedule incl. force majeure.
- **C7** Deposit/prepayment amounts and dates, card surcharges; we pay by bank transfer.
- **C8** One named overall coordinator from signature to final invoice, plus a named deputy for event days.

## What the venue's response must contain

- **Q1** Itemised quote grouped by section: quantity, unit price, days, total; each line marked included / optional / mandatory.
- **Q2** Separate list of every mandatory charge, supplier or staff role we didn't request (or a written "none").
- **Q3** A response to every numbered item: "yes", "yes at cost X" or "no, alternative Y" (a response form is included in the Complete PDF).
- **Q4** Floor plans with names and capacities, AV dossier, signage dossier, exhibitor handbook, rate card.
- **Q5** Draft agreement showing where this document and the response are incorporated.
- **Q6** Deposit/payment schedule, cancellation terms and minimums.

## Quick go / no-go checklist

Deal-breakers or near deal-breakers to check first for any candidate venue:

- [ ] Available for 4 consecutive days in late October (Tue–Fri or Mon–Thu) + Day 0 afternoon and Day 5 morning, avoiding holidays
- [ ] Plenary seating 900 (real chair count, R1)
- [ ] 3 × 300+ and 1 × 200+ breakouts on Days 1–3 (or plenary partitionable within 30 min)
- [ ] 5–6 rooms of 50–100 on Day 4
- [ ] Exhibition space for 12–15 stands of ~3 × 3 m near catering
- [ ] Lunch for 900 served within ~1 hour
- [ ] Wi-Fi for 1,800 devices, and own routers allowed (W6)
- [ ] Fully step-free with accessible toilets on every floor
- [ ] Hacker lounge, quiet room and lockable 25 m² office available
- [ ] Hotels with conference rates at or near the venue
- [ ] Willing to make the requirements document contractual and quote all foreseeable costs
