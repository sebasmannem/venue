## How venues were assessed

The hardest requirement is a **900-seat plenary plus four breakout tracks (3 × 300+ and 1 × 200+)** on Days 1–3. Many venues have one big hall, but few have four large sub-rooms. The spec allows the plenary room to double as one of the breakout tracks, so a venue needs at least:

- one room for ~900 (plenary, also used as a track), and
- two more rooms of 300+ and one of 200+.

Rejected venues fail this on room capacity alone. Other requirements (Wi-Fi, catering, accessibility, sponsors, hotels) have not been checked yet for any venue.

## Probably meet the requirements

### 1. RAI Amsterdam (Amsterdam Zuid)

The largest congress centre in the Netherlands; capacity is not an issue.

- **Plenary**: Auditorium, ~1,750 incl. balcony.
- **Breakouts**: Forum (~709 theatre), Elicium Ballroom (~2,000, divisible with sliding partitions), plus many smaller rooms.
- **Hotels**: several next to the venue.
- **To check**: cost for an event of our size, and other events in the building at the same time (A3, R2).
- Sources: [RAI rooms](https://rai.nl/en/organiser/your-location/rooms), [RAI Amsterdam (iamsterdam)](https://iamsterdam.com/en/conventions/venue-finder/rai-amsterdam)

### 2. De Doelen ICC (Rotterdam centre)

Schouwburgplein 50, a few minutes' walk from Rotterdam Centraal (~25 min by direct train from Schiphol). Outside Amsterdam.

- **Plenary**: Grote Zaal, 800–1,755.
- **Breakouts**: Willem Burger Zaal (fixed theatre seating, ~700), Jurriaanse Zaal (~465–523), Feestzaal (up to 450), plus 30+ sub-rooms of 20–270. That gives the plenary plus three 300+ tracks and a 200+ track.
- **Other space**: large foyers (Grote Zaal Foyer up to 1,500, Hal Grote Zaal up to 1,100) for sponsors and catering.
- **To check**: theatre-style seat counts for the Feestzaal and the largest sub-room, Day 4 rooms, hotels nearby (H2).
- Sources: [de Doelen ICC (locaties.nl)](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/venue/de_doelen_icc_rotterdam-184290), [Rotterdam event venues](https://www.rotterdam.info/en/state-of-the-art-event-venues-in-rotterdam-the-netherlands)

### 3. Rotterdam Ahoy (Rotterdam Zuid)

Large congress and exhibition centre in the south of Rotterdam, connected to the city centre by metro. Outside Amsterdam.

- **Plenary**: RTM Stage, 2,816 theatre (much larger than needed).
- **Breakouts**: 35 rooms in the Convention Centre, 50 to ~1,000 each.
- **Other space**: Expo Foyer ~2,300 m² for sponsors and catering.
- **To check**: how many rooms seat 300+ theatre, whether the RTM Stage can be scaled down for 900, other events on site (A3), hotels nearby (H2).
- Sources: [Ahoy floor plan](https://www.ahoy.nl/en/information/map), [Rotterdam Ahoy Convention Centre (locaties.nl)](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/space/rotterdam_ahoy_convention_centre-78413), [About RACC (Rotterdam Partners)](https://en.rotterdampartners.nl/?p=33588)

### 4. World Forum (The Hague)

International congress centre (used for UN and NATO conferences), ~30 minutes from Schiphol. Outside Amsterdam.

- **Plenary**: King Willem-Alexander auditorium, up to 2,161 theatre.
- **Breakouts**: 25–35 breakout rooms (sources disagree), directly connected to the auditorium.
- **Other space**: ~12,000 m² exhibition space.
- **To check**: breakout room sizes, any redevelopment plans (none found online), hotels nearby (H2).
- Sources: [World Forum (The Hague Convention Bureau)](https://thehague.com/business/en/node/184), [World Forum (AIPC)](https://aipc.org/member/world-forum-convention-center-the-hague/), [World Forum (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Forum_(The_Hague))

### 5. Jaarbeurs (Utrecht)

Large exhibition and congress centre next to Utrecht Centraal, so easy to reach by train. Outside Amsterdam.

- **Plenary**: Beatrix Theater, up to 1,500.
- **Breakouts**: 50+ conference rooms across several conference centres, up to ~650; Media Plaza across the road has 65 rooms of ~4–650, an auditorium of ~200 and the Polar area for ~650.
- **To check**: which rooms would be ours and how close they are to each other, other events in the building (A3), hotels nearby (H2).
- Sources: [Jaarbeurs Beatrix Theatre](https://jaarbeurs.nl/en/organiser/beatrix-theatre/theatre), [Jaarbeurs Congressen (eventplanner)](https://eventplanner.co.uk/directory/634_jaarbeurs-congressen.html), [Media Plaza (locaties.nl)](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/venue/congrescentrum_media_plaza-193704)

### 6. NH Noordwijk Conference Centre Leeuwenhorst (Noordwijkerhout)

Large conference hotel ~40 km from Amsterdam, near the coast. Outside Amsterdam.

- **Plenary**: Atrium, ~1,400 theatre (largest room ~989 m²).
- **Breakouts**: Rotonde 500–600 theatre, auditorium 150–250, ~120 meeting rooms in total, several combinable.
- **Hotel**: on site, so venue and accommodation in one place (H1).
- **To check**: current per-room theatre figures (listings disagree), travel from Schiphol / Amsterdam for international attendees.
- Sources: [Leeuwenhorst (onemeeting)](https://onemeeting.com/en/location/NH_Noordwijk_Conference_Centre_Leeuwenhorst/222), [Leeuwenhorst (meetings-conventions)](https://www.meetings-conventions.com/Meeting-Event-Venues/Noordwijkerhout-Netherlands/Convention-Hotel/NH-Noordwijk-Conference-Ctr-Leeuwenhorst-p51080075)

### 7. MECC Maastricht

Large congress and exhibition centre, but ~2.5 hours by train from Schiphol. Closer airports (Maastricht Aachen, Liège, Eindhoven, Düsseldorf, Brussels) have fewer direct flights.

- **Plenary**: two auditoria, up to 1,700 people.
- **Breakouts**: 30–55 breakout rooms (sources disagree); rooms have hosted meetings of 300.
- **Other space**: 2,500 m² expo foyer, 30,000 m² exhibition space.
- **To check**: theatre capacity per auditorium and per breakout room, travel for international attendees.
- Sources: [MECC Maastricht (Cvent)](https://cvent.com/venues/maastricht/exposition-center/mecc%20maastricht/venue-8992de04-c44e-400b-bef6-2ed339c9b985), [ESB 2023 venue](https://esbiomech.org/conference/esb2023/?p=196), [MECC (AEO)](https://www.aeo.org.uk/members/mecc-full)

## Borderline

### 8. Beurs van Berlage (Amsterdam)

Historic, sustainable congress centre in the centre of Amsterdam.

- **Plenary**: Grote Zaal, 1,300 theatre (1,600 max).
- **Breakouts**: Effectenbeurszaal (~750) and Graanbeurszaal (~800) give, with the Grote Zaal, three 300+ tracks. The fourth track (200+) is short: next largest are the Beursfoyer (200, but a foyer) and Berlagezaal, Keurzaal and Administratiezaal (150 each).
- **Hacker lounge (~25)**: Rode kamer (max. 35) or Roland Holst kamer (max. 30) *(PDF)*.
- **Quiet room**: one of the smaller committee rooms *(PDF)*.
- **To check**: what they can offer for the fourth room ("some flexibility on sizes" is allowed), step-free access in a historic building (Y1), room for 12–15 sponsor stands near catering, lunch for 900 within an hour, hotels nearby (H2).
- Sources: [Room overview](https://beursvanberlage.com/organise/room-overview/), [Effectenbeurszaal](https://beursvanberlage.com/organise/room-overview/effectenbeurszaal/), [Beurs van Berlage (iamsterdam)](https://acceptance.iamsterdam.com/en/conventions/venue-finder/amsterdam-conference-centre-beurs-van-berlage)

### 9. Hotel Okura (Amsterdam Zuid)

- **Plenary**: Grand Ballroom, 1,015 theatre, divisible into 5 sections.
- **Breakouts**: Heian I/II ~494; all other rooms ~155 or less.
- **Problem**: the 300+ tracks only work by splitting the ballroom, which then has to be rearranged for each plenary within the 30-minute break, and there is still no 200+ room. Same issue as Beurs van Berlage.
- Sources: [Hotel Okura meetings & events](https://okura.com/netherlands/amsterdam/hotel-okura-amsterdam/meetings-events/), [Hotel Okura (iamsterdam)](https://iamsterdam.com/en/conventions/venue-finder/hotel-okura-amsterdam)

## To check

### 10. Westergas (Amsterdam West)

Former gasworks turned into a venue complex in a park.

- **Plenary**: Gashouder, ~2,100 theatre (older factsheet), or Zuiveringshal West, ~1,000 theatre.
- **Breakouts**: De Wester (former Transformatorhuis), 500 theatre; Westergastheater, 250 theatre.
- **Concerns**: the halls are separate buildings, so attendees walk outside between talks in late October. The Gashouder was listed as under construction until 2026. Most figures come from older factsheets.
- Sources: [Westergasfabriek factsheet](https://www.uniquevenuesofamsterdam.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/English-Factsheet-Westergasfabriek-20161.pdf), [De Wester brochure 2026](https://uniquevenuesofamsterdam.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/De-Wester-Westergas-Brochure-2026-EN.pdf), [Gashouder](https://uniquevenuesofamsterdam.com/en/venue/gashouder/)

### 11. ijVENUES (Amsterdam, IJ waterfront)

Piet Heinkade 11, near Amsterdam Centraal.

- Listed with an auditorium, 20 breakout rooms, 4,000 m² exhibition space and a 408-room hotel on site; one listing says "up to 1,900 seated".
- **To check**: capacity per room. The address matches the Mövenpick hotel, and the name may cover several nearby venues.
- Source: [ijVENUES (hirespace)](https://hirespace.com/Spaces/Amsterdam/225719/IjVENUES/Meeting-Rooms/Events)

### 12. Johan Cruijff ArenA (Amsterdam Zuidoost)

- 22 rooms; Amsterdam '72 and Glasgow '72 Lounge up to 1,000, several rooms of 350–400.
- **To check**: those are maximums, not theatre seating, and the venue itself pitches conferences of 100–800.
- Sources: [Johan Cruijff ArenA congress](https://www.johancruijffarena.nl/en/business-events/congres/), [Johan Cruijff ArenA (locaties.nl)](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/venue/johan_cruijff_arena-8212)

### 13. TivoliVredenburg (Utrecht centre)

Concert venue next to Utrecht Centraal, a short walk from city-centre hotels. Outside Amsterdam.

- **Halls**: Grote Zaal ~1,700, Ronda ~2,000 (standing), Pandora ~625, Hertz ~543 seated, plus congress rooms.
- **Promising**: four large halls in one building, so a plenary plus 3 × 300+ tracks looks possible.
- **To check**: theatre seating per hall (concert figures differ), smaller rooms for Day 4 and the 200+ track, space for sponsors and catering for 900.
- Sources: [TivoliVredenburg (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TivoliVredenburg), [TivoliVredenburg halls](https://www.tivolivredenburg.nl/english/halls/), [TivoliVredenburg (locaties.nl)](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/venue/tivolivredenburg-13302)

### 14. NBC Congrescentrum (Nieuwegein)

Blokhoeve 1, Nieuwegein, south of Utrecht. Rebuilt after a fire in 2022. No hotel on site (suggests Van der Valk Utrecht and Fletcher Nieuwegein). Outside Amsterdam.

- **Halls**: Event Hall 300–1,750 (1,638 m²), Grand Hall 300–1,500 (1,515 m²), lounge 40–250.
- **Breakouts**: 22 sub-rooms of up to 100 (200 combined).
- **To check**: the 300+ tracks only work if the Grand Hall can be split into several soundproof rooms.
- Sources: [NBC (locaties.nl)](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/venue/nbc-99157), [NBC Congrescentrum (eventplanner)](https://www.eventplanner.net/directory/643_nbc-congrescentrum.html)

## Rejected

### 20. Circa Amsterdam

Event venue in the former Aviodome, Seineweg 2, Amsterdam.

- **Plenary**: 900 — just enough.
- **Breakouts**: 11 sub-rooms of 10–250 each, so no 300+ rooms. The PDF claimed 300+ rooms could be made by dividing foyers with movable walls; even then, acoustic separation would be a concern.
- Sources: [Circa Amsterdam (locaties.nl)](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/venue/circa_amsterdam-14628), [Circa Evenementenzaal (hirespace)](https://hirespace.com/Spaces/Amsterdam/225196/Circa-Amsterdam/Evenementenzaal/Events)

### 21. LIEF (Amsterdam)

- **Plenary**: The Main, 700 theatre — the very bottom of the 700–900 range.
- **Breakouts**: The Arch (300) and The Pit (~200), then rooms of 50 or less, so one 300+ room is missing.
- Sources: [LIEF Amsterdam (locaties.nl)](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/venue/lief_amsterdam-11786), [The Main](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/space/the_main-27540)

### 22. Postillion Amsterdam

Hotel + convention centre at Paul van Vlissingenstraat 9-11, Amsterdam-Oost (not Zuid), 252 hotel rooms (another listing says 191).

- **Plenary**: rooms 1–8 + corridor, 1,200 theatre.
- **Breakouts**: the plenary hall is 8 sections of ~121 seats; the best split is 2 × 450. All other rooms seat ~75 or fewer, so a plenary plus 4 tracks isn't possible.
- Sources: [Postillion (conferencehotelgroup.com)](https://www.conferencehotelgroup.com/hotels/1332), [Postillion (locaties.nl)](https://www.locaties.nl/en-US/venue/postillion_hotel_convention_centre_amsterdam-13882)

### 23. Tropenmuseum / KIT (Amsterdam Oost)

- **Plenary**: largest theatre room is the Grote Zaal at 445; Marmeren Zaal holds 600 max (not theatre). No room near 900.
- Sources: [KIT meeting & events (iamsterdam)](https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/conventions/venue-finder/kit-meeting-events-dinner-location), [KIT conferences (iamsterdam)](https://iamsterdam.com/en/conventions/venue-finder/kit-royal-tropical-institute-conferences-events)

## Alternative approach: event hall with built breakout rooms

*(PDF)* If a venue has no four fixed 300+ rooms, a common solution at this size is a large event or expo hall where a stand builder (for example The Inside Standbouw) builds soundproofed breakout rooms of 100–500 people with their own AV and lighting. This opens up larger halls (Inspyrium is named as an example; EXPO Haarlemmermeer is an obvious hall near Amsterdam, not checked yet; Expo Houten (#38) has 1,850–5,500 m² halls next to a 350-seat congress room). The extra costs must be quoted up front (C2), and acoustic separation needs checking.
