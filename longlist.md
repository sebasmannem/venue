## Biggest challenge

The PDF points out that the hardest requirement is **four breakout rooms of 300+** next to a 900-seat plenary. Many venues have one big hall, but few have four large sub-rooms. Check this first for every candidate (see R1: real chair counts, not brochure maximums).

## Venues

### 1. Beurs van Berlage (Amsterdam)

Historic, sustainable congress centre in the centre of Amsterdam with 21–25 multifunctional rooms *(PDF)*.

- **Plenary (900)**: Grote Zaal (max. 1,600) or Effectenbeurszaal (800+, depending on layout) *(PDF)*.
- **4 × 300+ breakouts**: a combination of the large halls (Effectenbeurszaal, Graanbeurszaal) and larger sub-rooms (Berlage zaal, Administratiezaal), or splitting larger spaces with soundproof walls *(PDF)*.
- **Hacker lounge (~25)**: Rode kamer (max. 35) or Roland Holst kamer (max. 30), with comfortable furniture, power and screens *(PDF)*.
- **Quiet room**: one of the smaller, quietly located committee rooms *(PDF)*.
- **To check**: real seat counts per room, step-free access throughout a historic building (Y1), room for 12–15 sponsor stands near catering, lunch for 900 within an hour, and hotels nearby (H2).

### 2. Circa Amsterdam (Amsterdam)

Modern, "100% circular" event venue in the former Aviodome dome, 5,000 m² in total *(PDF)*.

- **Plenary (900)**: central Black Box, 900–1,000 in theatre layout *(PDF)*.
- **4 × 300+ breakouts**: 11 flexible sub-rooms; 300+ rooms would be made by dividing foyers and sub-spaces with plug-and-play movable walls *(PDF)*.
- **Hacker lounge (~25)**: one of the sub-rooms set up as a boardroom; power at every seat *(PDF)*.
- **Quiet room**: one of the smaller sub-rooms (from 10 people) kept outside the programme *(PDF)*.
- **To check**: whether 4 × 300+ is realistic within 5,000 m² alongside the plenary, exhibition and catering; acoustic separation of the divided zones; location and public transport; hotels nearby (H2).

### 3. LIEF (Amsterdam)

- **To check**: capacity of the main hall and number/size of sub-rooms, Day 4 rooms, accessibility, location and hotels nearby.

### 4. RAI Amsterdam (Amsterdam Zuid)

- **First impression** *(to verify)*: the largest congress centre in the Netherlands, so capacity is unlikely to be the problem. Hotels are next to the venue.
- **To check**: cost for an event of our size, and whether we'd share the building with other events (A3, R2).

### 5. Postillion Amsterdam

- Hotel venue, so a conference room rate applies (H1).
- **To check**: exact location (listed as "Amsterdam Zuid"), size of the largest room, whether 4 × 300+ breakouts are possible, and how many hotel rooms are available.

### 6. Tropenmuseum (Amsterdam Oost)

- **To check**: a 900-seat plenary and 4 × 300+ breakouts in a museum building seem unlikely, so check capacity first. Also check step-free access, exhibition space and catering for 900.

### 7. Van der Valk Breukelen

- Hotel venue near the A2 between Amsterdam and Utrecht *(to verify)*. A conference room rate applies (H1).
- **To check**: capacity of the largest hall and breakouts, public transport access for international attendees.

### 8. Jaarbeurs (Utrecht)

- **First impression** *(to verify)*: large exhibition and congress centre next to Utrecht Centraal, so easy to reach by train.
- **To check**: congress room sizes, sharing the building with other events (A3), and hotels nearby.

### 9. Van der Valk Utrecht

- Hotel venue, so a conference room rate applies (H1).
- **To check**: capacity of the largest hall and breakouts, public transport access.

## Alternative approach: event hall with built breakout rooms

*(PDF)* If a venue has no four fixed 300+ rooms, a common solution at this size is a large event or expo hall where a stand builder (for example The Inside Standbouw) builds soundproofed breakout rooms of 100–500 people with their own AV and lighting. This opens up larger halls (Inspyrium is named as an example). The extra costs must be quoted up front (C2), and acoustic separation needs checking.

## Open questions from the PDF

The PDF ended with questions that also help narrow the search:

- **Region**: Amsterdam only, or also Utrecht / central Netherlands? (The list currently has both.)
- **Breakout rooms**: must they be fully soundproof, or are separated zones in a larger hall acceptable?
- **Hotel**: should hotel rooms be directly linked to the venue? (Hotel venue → H1, congress centre → H2.)
